Employee Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to annual leave, sick leave, and casual leave according to company policy.

Annual leave must be requested through the HR system before the planned absence whenever possible. Managers may approve or reject requests based on team availability and business requirements.

Employees who are unable to attend work because of illness should inform their manager as soon as possible. Sick leave requests may require supporting documentation depending on the duration of the absence.

The HR department is responsible for maintaining employee leave records and answering questions related to leave balances and company policies.

Employees should contact HR if they need clarification regarding leave eligibility, approval procedures, or available leave balances.
